# Ecosystem Partner Features

## Who Qualifies
- Technology Business Incubators (TBIs)
- Corporate Incubators
- Accelerators
- Angel Networks
- Venture Capital Firms
- Multilateral / ASEAN Partners

---

## Onboarding

- Selects organization type from the categories above
- Sets sector focus (target industries)
- Sets target regions
- Sets stage preference (Pre-seed through Late-Stage)
- Provides 3 verifiable references (colleagues, portfolio founders, co-investors)

---

## Profile Page

Displays the following after onboarding:
- Organization Type
- Sector Focus
- Target Regions
- Stage Preference

---

## Demo Day Access (June 21, SMX — Philippine Blockchain Week)

- **Judge Panel Seat** — scores all 10 startup pitches using Exoasia's 100-point rubric (Problem & Market 25%, Solution & Tech 25%, Business Model 25%, Team & Traction 25%); scores count toward official ranking
- **Deal Room 1:1s** — post-pitch sessions with up to 5 chosen startups; first-look before competing investors; available for co-investment, partnership, or incubation discussions
- **Startup Nominations** — nominate 1–2 portfolio companies for Demo Day consideration with expedited review
- **ANP – Bit Angels MOU Signing** — invitation to witness the ceremony during score tabulation
- **Networking Night** — exclusive offsite event near SMX after the 2-hour Demo Day program; curated guest list of founders, investors, sponsors, and speakers

---

## Matching Platform (`/ecosystem`)

- **Curated Startup Pipeline** — browse and filter startups by domain, stage, fundraising target, and technology stack; all listings are pre-screened
- **Mandate-Based Matching** — platform surfaces startups matching the partner's organization type, stage preference, and sector focus; notifies when new matching startups join
- **Direct Founder Connection** — request introductions through the platform; founder profiles include EVODINE™-based readiness assessment
- **Cohort Nomination Rights** — TBIs and accelerators can nominate platform-registered startups directly into their next cohort without a separate intake
- **Future Founders Arena Events** — first-access invitations to all future Demo Days, PBW events, and Exoasia-organized investment convenings
- **Exoasia Deal Flow Reports** — quarterly summaries of new platform startups, sector trends, and investment activity across the Philippine Web3 + AI ecosystem

---

## Deal Flow Command Center (`/ecosystem` portal)

- **Portfolio Overview** — stats grid (total companies, projects, intros, active matches, pending, stale alerts) and live company feed
- **Nominate for Demo Day** — invite portfolio companies by email; 14-day token, expedited review
- **Co-Pilot Kanban** — visual board of match statuses with stale alerts (7+ days of inactivity)
- **Company Deep-Dive** — per-company panel showing mandate-matched opportunities, match timeline, fit scores, and intro statuses (Pending → Intro Sent → Active → Introduced)
- **Intervene on Stale Matches** — reset stale match timestamps to re-activate a stalled introduction

---

## Dashboard

- **Deal Flow Command Center widget** — quick-access tiles for portfolio overview and Co-Pilot Kanban
- CTA: Nominate for Demo Day and track mandate-matched deal flow

---

## Navigation

Gets access to: Portfolio (`/ecosystem`), Events (`/events`), Community (`/community`)

---

## Data Access (Row-Level Security)

- Can read portfolio companies' projects, match scores, matches, and deal cards
- Scoped strictly to startups in the partner's active portfolio
- No access to data for startups outside their portfolio
