# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Startups (founders seeking capital), investors (angels and funds deploying capital), and ecosystem partners (accelerators and incubators managing startup portfolios) operating in the Philippines and Southeast Asia. Advisors and admins manage the platform and curate matches. Users are invite-only and verified before gaining full access — they arrive with intent, not curiosity.

## Product Purpose

Founders Arena is a private B2B matching platform that connects curated startups with investors and ecosystem partners. It runs a two-sided pipeline: startups list investment projects, investors score them via AI, and both parties initiate or accept introductions. Success means fewer cold outreaches and more warm, high-signal introductions that convert.

## Brand Personality

Premium, urgent, ambitious. The platform feels earned — like a private network where access itself signals credibility. Energy is high-stakes but controlled, not chaotic. The tone is confident and direct, not salesy or hype-driven.

## Anti-references

Corporate BI tools (Tableau, Power BI, enterprise gray dashboards): data-heavy with zero personality, soulless metric grids, lifeless typography. This platform handles real capital and real ambition — it should feel nothing like an internal analytics tool.

Also avoid: generic blue-sidebar SaaS clones (Linear, Stripe clone energy), crypto neon chaos (glowing purple/cyan gradients), and social feed energy (notification spam, LinkedIn-style activity walls).

## Design Principles

1. **Earned access, earned trust.** Every surface should reinforce that being here means something. Curation and exclusivity show in the craft.
2. **Signal over noise.** Members are busy operators. Surface the most important action or signal first; suppress everything secondary.
3. **Tension before resolution.** The brand holds tangerine-to-hot-pink tension well. Use contrast and color deliberately to create urgency without anxiety.
4. **Dark as identity.** The dark canvas is not a mode — it is the product. Do not soften it into generic dark SaaS gray.
5. **Precision over decoration.** JetBrains Mono labels, tight spacing, and exact borders communicate that this is a serious tool built by people who care.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

WCAG AA compliance: 4.5:1 contrast ratio for body text, 3:1 for large/bold text. Full keyboard navigation and screen reader support. All animations must have `prefers-reduced-motion` alternatives (crossfade or instant transition, not removal). Focus indicators must be visible and styled on-brand.
